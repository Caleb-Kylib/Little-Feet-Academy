Honest conversation improves outcomes. CBC asks more of planning, materials, and collaboration—so bottlenecks are normal until systems mature.

Time and workload

Designing tasks, rubrics, and follow-up feedback takes longer than lecturing from notes alone. Strong schools protect common planning time and share banks of activities so teachers are not starting from zero each week.

Resources

Practical learning needs safe space and tools—from manipulatives to simple lab materials. Communities that fundraise transparently and prioritise durable kits often see steadier implementation.

Professional development

One-off workshops help less than ongoing coaching. Look for schools that invest in:

  • Lesson study or peer observation cycles
  • Assessment literacy for families
  • Inclusive strategies for mixed-ability classes

What parents can do

Ask how to support practice at home without doing the project for your child. Pair school expectations with routines—reading aloud, measuring while cooking, discussing news—to reinforce competencies naturally.
