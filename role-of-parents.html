The Role of Parents in a CBC Journey

By Little Feet Academy · 22 January 2026

  1. Home
  2. Articles
  3. Role of parents

You are your child’s first teacher. Under CBC, family partnership means reinforcing habits and curiosity—while teachers lead curriculum and assessment design.

Ask clarifying questions

Instead of “did you get homework?”, try “what skill are you practising this week?” or “what did you revise after feedback?” Those prompts align with competency language and help children reflect.

Create routines

Consistent sleep, reading time, and a calm workspace matter more than expensive gadgets. Routines protect focus so classroom learning carries into home life without burnout.

Support—without taking over

When projects feel big, model planning: break tasks into steps, gather materials, then step back so your child owns the work. Teachers notice authenticity—and learners keep confidence in their own capacity.

Stay in touch

Share observations from home—attention patterns, interests, worries—in parent meetings. Small insights help teachers differentiate support and celebrate growth you see outside school walls.
