About Little Feet Academy

A warm school community in the heart of Rongai

We are a caring Christian-based academy serving families in Ongata Rongai and beyond. Our teachers blend the Competency-Based Curriculum with purposeful play, creativity, and strong values—so every child feels known, challenged, and celebrated.

From early years through upper primary pathways, we focus on confidence, character, and curiosity: the foundations that last long after the school day ends.

  • Small-group attention and clear communication with families
  • Safe, joyful classrooms and enriching co-curricular activities
  • Partnership with parents—you’re part of the journey
