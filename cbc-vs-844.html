Many parents experienced 8-4-4 themselves; understanding CBC is easier when we compare aims and classroom habits, not only labels.

Different centres of gravity

8-4-4 often leaned on syllabus coverage and summative exams as the main signal of success. CBC still values knowledge—but asks how learners use it in tasks, projects, and reflections mapped to competencies.

Teaching and assessment

  • 8-4-4: heavier weight on end-of-term papers; teaching sometimes paced to those peaks.
  • CBC: formative assessment woven through units; portfolios and rubrics document growth over time.

Pathways and transitions

Junior secondary under CBC introduces pathway thinking earlier—helping learners connect strengths with realistic next steps. That does not narrow possibilities; it widens informed choice when guidance is done well.

For families

Open conversations with teachers matter in both systems. Under CBC, asking “what competency is my child working toward?” and “how can we practise at home?” aligns neatly with how lessons are planned.
