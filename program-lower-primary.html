Lower Primary

Grades 1 – 3

Inquiry, literacy, and numeracy come together in a classroom culture where every learner is known and challenged.

About the Program

Lower Primary is where reading fluency, writing confidence, and mathematical reasoning take off. We use CBC learning areas and a blend of direct teaching, small-group work, and projects so children learn to think clearly and communicate with purpose.

Grade range: Grade 1 – Grade 3  |  Approach: structured inquiry, continuous assessment, and integration of values and digital citizenship in age-appropriate ways.

Key Features

Academic strength with room for joy, movement, and creativity.

Language & Communication

Reading, writing, and oral skills developed through text sets, class discussions, and short presentations.

Mathematics & Problem Solving

Concrete-representational-abstract strategies so learners can explain their thinking, not just give answers.

Science & Environmental Studies

Hands-on activities that develop observation, data sense, and care for the environment.

Creative Arts & PE

Art, music, and physical education support motor skills, confidence, and cultural appreciation.

Digital & Media Literacy

Guided, supervised use of devices to research, create, and practise responsible online behaviour.

Formative Support

Differentiated support and feedback so all learners are stretched appropriately.

Curriculum & Activities

Subjects, activities, and skills that prepare learners for upper primary and beyond.

Core Subjects

Language, mathematics, science, social studies, and religious education are taught with clear learning outcomes, literacy across the day, and cross-curricular projects that connect ideas to real life in Kenya and the wider world.

Activities & Co-curricular

Clubs, sports, service tasks, and house activities build leadership, fair play, and school spirit. Learners set goals, reflect on performance, and celebrate effort alongside achievement.

Skills for Upper Primary

We strengthen study habits, note-taking routines, and academic vocabulary. Critical reading and written explanations are practised in every term so the transition to higher grades is smooth.

Core Competencies

CBC-style competencies for Lower Primary, embedded in day-to-day learning.

Literacy & Numeracy

Fluency in reading, writing, and number sense for all tasks.

Communication

Clear speaking, active listening, and audience-aware writing.

Critical Thinking

Asking “why?”, comparing ideas, and justifying solutions.

Creativity

Designing, making, and innovating in projects and problem tasks.

Digital Literacy

Safe, purposeful use of technology for learning and creation.
