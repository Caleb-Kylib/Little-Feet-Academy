Junior Secondary

Grades 7 & beyond

Rigorous academics, real-world projects, and guidance for the pathways that come next.

About the Program

Junior Secondary learners engage with more specialised knowledge and skills while developing study habits, leadership, and digital fluency. Our teaching balances national curriculum expectations with authentic tasks—lab work, research, and collaborative projects.

Level: Junior Secondary (from Grade 7)  |  Approach: problem-based learning, career-linked exposure, and strong pastoral and academic mentoring.

Key Features

A challenging yet supported environment for adolescents to grow in skill and character.

STEM & Practical Work

Laboratory and integrated science tasks that build inquiry, safety, and evidence-based reasoning.

Digital & Pre-technical Skills

Computational thinking, digital creation, and responsible technology use in learning and projects.

Languages & Communication

Strong focus on writing for purpose, public speaking, and academic discourse across subjects.

Leadership & Co-curricular

Debate, clubs, and house systems that grow confidence and responsibility outside class time.

Pathways Guidance

Mentoring conversations to align strengths with subject choices and future study options.

Values & Wellbeing

Explicit focus on integrity, service, and healthy peer relationships in the teen years.

Curriculum & Activities

Integrated subjects, activities, and transferable skills for senior school readiness.

Core & Integrated Learning

Mathematics, languages, humanities, and sciences are taught for depth and application. Longer assignments build research skills, referencing habits, and academic writing.

Activities & Real-world Skills

Community projects, enterprise-style tasks, and team presentations help learners connect classroom ideas to life beyond school. Sport and the arts continue to shape teamwork and self-discipline.

Readiness for Senior Pathways

We help learners set academic goals, manage workload, and reflect on progress. Career talks and subject clinics support informed next steps in line with national standards.

Core Competencies

Competencies for Junior Secondary, embedded across subjects and co-curricular life.

Critical Thinking

Analysing sources, testing solutions, and defending conclusions with evidence.

Communication & Collaboration

Productive group work, academic dialogue, and respectful feedback.

Digital Literacy

Using devices and media ethically for research, design, and communication.

Self-efficacy

Goal-setting, persistence, and ownership of learning outcomes.

Citizenship & Ethics

Responsibility, service, and integrity in school and the wider community.
