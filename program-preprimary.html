Pre-Primary

PP1 & PP2

Structured, joyful learning that builds literacy, numeracy, and confidence before Grade 1.

About the Program

Pre-Primary bridge play with purposeful classroom routines. We follow CBC early-years pathways so learners build language, number sense, and environmental understanding while still enjoying music, art, and movement every week.

Ages / levels: 4 – 6 years (PP1 & PP2)  |  Approach: inquiry, hands-on projects, and continuous formative assessment in a positive classroom culture.

Key Features

Strong foundations, gentle pacing, and plenty of room for curiosity.

Phonics & Early Literacy

Systematic sound–letter work, vocabulary building, and story-based reading confidence.

Foundational Numeracy

Counting, number operations, measurement, and pattern tasks using real objects and games.

Environmental Activities

Exploring nature, health, and the local environment through projects and field-friendly tasks.

Arts & Movement

Music, creative arts, and physical play that strengthen expression and motor coordination.

Collaboration

Group tasks and presentations that build listening, turn-taking, and respect for others.

CBC-Aligned Progress

Competency-focused feedback so families see growth across learning areas, not just scores.

Curriculum & Activities

Subjects, activities, and skills woven together for holistic readiness for Lower Primary.

Core Learning Areas

Language, mathematics, and environmental activities are taught through a mix of direct instruction, stations, and short projects. Religious education and life skills are integrated into weekly themes to support values and self-awareness.

Enrichment & Skills

Co-curricular clubs, library time, and digital readiness tasks introduce age-appropriate technology while prioritizing eye health and supervised screen use. Learners practice presenting ideas, asking questions, and working in pairs or small groups.

Readiness for Grade 1

We strengthen independence—packing bags, following timetables, and completing small responsibilities—so the step into Lower Primary feels natural and exciting.

Core Competencies

Foundational competencies for the early years, aligned to CBC goals.

Communication

Speaking, listening, and early print awareness.

Motor Development

Writing, cutting, and coordinated movement in play.

Social Interaction

Teamwork, respect, and classroom citizenship.

Creativity

Imaginative play, art, and open-ended problem solving.

Emotional Growth

Identifying feelings, self-regulation, and resilience.
