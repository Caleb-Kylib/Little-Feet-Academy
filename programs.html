Competency-Based Curriculum

Kenyan CBC Excellence

We pride ourselves on an exemplary implementation of the Competency-Based Curriculum. We don't just teach for exams; we equip students with the skills required for the 21st century.

  • Integrated Learning Areas

    Language, mathematics, environmental studies, and psycho-motor activities work together in meaningful ways.

  • Values & Core Competencies

    Fostering communication, collaboration, and self-efficacy in every learner.

  • Continuous Assessment

    Tracking growth through projects and observations rather than relying on one final test.

Beyond the Classroom

Our co-curricular program helps learners build confidence, creativity, leadership, and life skills through meaningful, real-world experiences.

Sports & Athletics

From team games to fitness challenges, learners build discipline, resilience, and teamwork while staying active and healthy.

Music & Drama

Learners express themselves through performance, storytelling, and rhythm, building creativity, confidence, and stage presence.

Debate Club

Students learn to think critically, organize ideas, and speak persuasively while respecting different perspectives.

Science Club

Through experiments and inquiry projects, children explore how things work and develop a love for discovery.

Art & Design

Creative studios and hands-on projects help learners strengthen imagination, fine motor skills, and visual communication.

Scouts Club

Service initiatives and outreach activities nurture empathy, responsibility, and a strong spirit of citizenship.
