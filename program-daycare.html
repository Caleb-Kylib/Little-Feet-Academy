Early Years

Daycare

A calm, joyful first school experience where little ones learn through play, routine, and love.

About the Program

Our Daycare is designed for children who are taking their first steps into a group learning environment. We focus on emotional safety, gentle routines, and hands-on play that supports early language, movement, and social growth.

Ages: 2 – 4 years  |  Approach: play-based, sensory-rich, and child-centred, aligned with early years best practice.

Key Features

Everything we do is designed to help toddlers feel safe, curious, and ready to connect with the world around them.

Caring, Safe Environment

Low ratios, supervised play areas, and consistent routines that help children build trust.

Creative Play & Art

Messy play, music, and craft activities that develop fine motor skills and self-expression.

Social & Language Growth

Songs, stories, and group games that encourage turn-taking, listening, and early communication.

Movement & Motor Skills

Indoor and outdoor movement sessions that build balance, coordination, and body awareness.

Parent Partnership

Clear communication with families so home and school support the same healthy habits and milestones.

Nourishment & Routines

Snacks, rest time, and hygiene routines that promote wellbeing and school readiness over time.

Curriculum & Activities

A blend of guided experiences that prepare children for pre-primary with confidence and curiosity.

Foundational Experiences

Circle time, story sessions, and structured free-play help children build attention span, vocabulary, and a love of books. We weave early maths ideas into games—sorting, counting, and pattern play.

Indoor & Outdoor Play

Outdoor play supports gross motor development; indoor corners include sensory bins, building blocks, and role-play. Skills developed include sharing, problem-solving, and self-regulation in a peer group.

Holistic Readiness

We help children grow independence—dressing, packing bags, and following simple instructions—so they transition smoothly to PP1 when the time is right.

Core Competencies

Early competencies that set the stage for lifelong learning.

Communication Skills

Listening, speaking, and non-verbal expression through play.

Motor Skills

Fine and gross motor control through age-appropriate tasks.

Social Interaction

Cooperation, empathy, and healthy friendships.

Creativity

Imagination and exploration through art and dramatic play.

Emotional Development

Naming feelings, self-soothing, and building resilience.
