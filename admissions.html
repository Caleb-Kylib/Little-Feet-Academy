Admission Process

A simple, transparent path to joining Little Feet Academy.

Fill Application Form

Visit the school to pick a form or download the school fees brochure. Fill it in and submit at our front office.

Interview At School

We will schedule a physical interview for your child to understand their unique needs and placement.

Interview Feedback

Our team will review the interview results and provide detailed feedback to the parents within 48 hours.

Successful applicants will be invited to pick up their official admission letter and joining instructions.

Download School Fees Brochure (PDF) Talk to Admissions

Online Application

Please provide the details below to begin the official enrollment process. Our admissions team will review your application and contact you within 24-48 hours.

Application Tips

  • Ensure child's name matches birth certificate.
  • Provide a valid phone number for SMS updates.
  • Double-check email for the confirmation.
